# city-day-night

900×500 canvas, 16s seamless loop of a city skyline going from day to night and back. The sky gradient shifts day → sunset → night → dawn → day. The sun sets behind the skyline and the moon rises. At least 8 buildings of varied heights. At dusk at least 40 windows light up one by one in a scattered (not left-to-right) order, and they switch off before dawn. Stars fade in and twinkle at night. A car drives along a road in front of the buildings, and its headlights turn on at night. Everything must line up at the loop seam.

## Change

Change the whole cycle from 16s to 20s, keeping every phase in proportion and the loop seamless. Add a second car driving right-to-left in a lane closer to the viewer, with headlights that turn on at night like the first car. Make it rain from the middle of the night until dawn: rain fades in, falls continuously, and fades out before day.
