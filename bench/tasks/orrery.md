# orrery

500×500 canvas, seamless 12s loop. A glowing sun at the centre. Four planets on visible circular orbit rings, each a distinct colour, with orbital periods that divide 12s evenly (e.g. 2s, 3s, 4s, 6s) so the loop is seamless. The third planet has a moon orbiting it. Each planet has a small text label that travels with it and stays upright (does not rotate). A counter in the top-left corner reads "Day N", counting up from 0 to 365 over the loop.
