# bouncing-ball

600×300 canvas, 4s seamless loop. A red ball enters from the left, bounces three times with decreasing height across the floor, and exits right. At each impact it squashes (wider, shorter), then stretches on the way up. A soft elliptical shadow on the floor grows and darkens as the ball nears the floor. Small dust puffs appear at each impact and fade. Simple background: sky and a floor line.
