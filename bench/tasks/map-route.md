# map-route

A 700×420, 8s scene: a stylised map (a few rounded land shapes and roads drawn as paths), a route line draws from A to B along a curvy path, a car marker follows the line exactly and rotates to its heading, and an ETA label counts down from 12 min to 0.

## Change

Make the route longer: add a third stop C after B with its own marker and label; the car pauses 0.5s at B, then continues to C, and the ETA counts down to 0 at C instead of B. Keep the total duration.
